# SwaraCoach — User Guide

*(Add screenshots from `Docs/Screenshots/` where marked 📷 before exporting to PDF.)*

## 1. Before you start
- Use **headphones**. Without them the guide tone is picked up by the microphone and confuses the scoring.
- Sing in a **quiet room**, about a hand's width from the phone.
- SwaraCoach never records or uploads your voice. Everything is analysed on your phone.

## 2. First launch 📷
Three short screens explain the app. On the last one tap **Allow microphone & start** and choose *Allow*. If you tap *Don't Allow*, you can still look around; turn the microphone on later in Settings → SwaraCoach.

## 3. Singing a song 📷
1. **Sing** tab → choose *Songs* or *Exercises* → tap a title.
2. The song page shows difficulty, range and your past attempts. Tap **Start singing**.
3. Tap **Start**. Purple bars are the notes to sing; they move towards the vertical line. Your voice is the white line. The big note name shows what you are singing, with *flat*, *sharp* or *in tune* and the number of cents (100 cents = one semitone).
4. If you talk instead of sing, a hint appears: *We hear talking. Try singing "aah".*
5. When the song ends, the **Results** screen shows your score out of 100, stars, the percentage of time you were in tune and your average error. Tap **Try again** or **Done**.

**Tip:** singing the same melody one octave higher or lower is fine — it still counts as in tune.

## 4. Daily warm-up
The card at the top of the *Sing* tab starts the *Sa Re Ga Ma* swara scale, a good 1-minute warm-up.

## 5. Tuner 📷
Open the **Tuner** tab and sing or hum one note. The needle shows how many cents sharp or flat you are. When the needle is in the green zone you feel a small vibration. The chip under the gauge says **Singing detected** when the phone recognises singing.

## 6. Voice range test 📷
**Progress** tab → *Your voice range*. Sing your lowest comfortable note for 2 seconds on "aah", then your highest. SwaraCoach shows your range and voice type (bass, baritone, tenor, alto or soprano) and can save it.

## 7. Progress 📷
Streak (days in a row), average score this week, a chart of your scores (pinch with two fingers to zoom the date range) and a list of attempts. Swipe an attempt left to delete it.

## 8. Accessibility
Supports Dark Mode, larger text sizes (at the largest sizes the graph is replaced by a bigger readout), VoiceOver, and *Reduce Motion*.

## 9. FAQ
- **The line jumps around.** Move closer, reduce background noise, use headphones.
- **My score is 0.** The phone heard no singing. Check the microphone permission; try the Tuner to see if it reacts to you.
- **Why is the app not scoring when I speak?** By design: only singing is scored.
